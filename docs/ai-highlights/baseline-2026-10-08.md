## What the baseline tells us

- Real analysis completed in approximately nine seconds in the captured log.
- It produced 64 bar marks, 255 beats, and 715 full-mix onsets after deduplication. Self-similarity analysis reported three repetition groups.
- It produced zero hierarchy section marks and zero energy curves. The review/story path supplied a fallback section for theme assignment.
- It produced zero energy impacts, drops, gaps, crashes, and riff bursts. The logged reasons include unavailable `bbc_energy`, missing structure/harmony plugins, and missing drum stems.
- Existing variant-library validation warnings were emitted during generation; the export still completed. Keep them visible in the capture log rather than presenting the baseline as warning-free.

**Interpretation:** zero special-moment marks in this run cannot establish that the recording has no special sounds, nor measure detector recall. It demonstrates that a general highlights detector must define useful full-mix behavior when optional components are missing. This also validates the need to record capability profiles alongside comparisons.

No exceptional sound has yet been listener-annotated. No highlight has been traced to visible lighting. No screenshot, FSEQ, or video render was produced. Do not use this result as a perceptual-quality pass.
